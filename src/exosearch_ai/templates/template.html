Threats to validity

Disposition outcomes may reflect follow-up selection bias. Catalog parameters can change after confirmation. The TFOPWG label is an observational status, not a direct measurement of physical ground truth. Candidate PC/APC samples remain unlabeled in training.

Interpretation boundary

Model output measures pattern similarity within this training setup. It does not independently discover planets, confirm astrophysical phenomena, or replace expert follow-up observations. For genuine publication, freeze a dated NASA snapshot and independently reproduce the experiment on it. Historic performance can be optimistic if parameter values reflect later follow-up measurements.
